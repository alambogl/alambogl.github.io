Activity Bucket List

Skydiving

Description: Tandem skydiving involves jumping out of an aircraft at 10,000 to 14,000 feet while harnessed to a certified instructor, experiencing up to 60 seconds of adrenaline-pumping freefall followed by a scenic parachute descent.

Why It Interests Me: Growing up my dad has told me stories about skydiving and how thrilling it must be to experience the freefall and the adrenaline rush, making it a dream activity for me.

Average Cost: $200 – $300 for a tandem jump ($300 – $400+ if adding photo/video packages).

Bungee Jumping

Description: Free-falling from a massive structure like a bridge, crane, or cliff while attached to a large elastic cord, experiencing a thrill of acceleration followed by dramatic bounces above the ground or water below.

Why It Interests Me: Similar to skydiving, bungee jumping offers an exhilarating freefall experience and a rush of adrenaline, making it an exciting activity for thrill-seekers like me.

Average Cost: $100 – $250 per jump (varies by location and height).

San Diego Comic-Con

Description: The ultimate pop-culture convention held annually at the San Diego Convention Center, bringing together comic book creators, movie stars, video game developers, exclusive panels, collectibles, and incredible cosplay.

Why It Interests Me: I'm a huge nerd when it comes to comic books and superheros and have been to a few local conventions so being able to go to the biggest and most prestigious comic-con event in the world is incredibly exciting for me.

Average Cost: $1,200 – $2,500+ total (includes ~$300–$350 for 4-day badges, plus travel, hotel, food, and merch).
