Location Bucket List

Welcome to my location bucket list! Here you can explore a breakdown of world destinations I dream of visiting, complete with landmark highlights, heritage connections, and estimated travel expenses.

Location Media Description Sites to See Reason for Including Average Cost
Japan An island nation in East Asia blending rich ancient traditions, historic temples, and futuristic neon-lit metropolis cities. Mount Fuji, Tokyo Shibuya Crossing, Kyoto Shrines, Osaka Dotonbori I know japan is the token place for nerds to visit but aside from that the culture and history have always fascinated me. $2,500 – $4,500 total (10–14 days incl. flights, accommodation, JR pass, and food)
Italy A Southern European country known for its Mediterranean coastline, iconic historic architecture, world-renowned cuisine, and artistic heritage. Colosseum in Rome, Venice Canals, Florence Duomo, Amalfi Coast I'm part italian but have no direct connection to that part of my family and have always been curious about exploring its rich history and culture and possibly connecting with that side of my family. $2,200 – $4,000 total (10–12 days incl. flights, trains, lodging, and food)
China A vast East Asian country with a recorded history spanning thousands of years, featuring dramatic natural landscapes and sprawling modern cities. Great Wall of China, Forbidden City in Beijing, Terracotta Army in Xi'an, Guilin Karst Mountains Aside from its vast culture and history, China's modern cities and technological advancements also have intrested me and I am eager to experience the blend of tradition and modernity firsthand. $2,000 – $3,800 total (10–14 days incl. flights, visa fees, tours, and lodging)
Egypt A transcontinental country spanning the northeast corner of Africa, famous for ancient civilization landmarks along the fertile Nile River Valley. Great Pyramids of Giza, Sphinx, Karnak Temple in Luxor, Nile River Cruise Ever since I was a kid and learned about the ancient world in egypt it's always stuck with me as a place i want to visit and explore its history and culture. $1,800 – $3,200 total (7–10 days incl. flights, guided tour packages, and lodging)
