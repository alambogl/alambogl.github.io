Personal Goals

Welcome to my personal goals bucket list! This section highlights practical, hands-on skills and physical disciplines I aim to learn and master over time.

Functional 3D Printing

Description: Designing and manufacturing real-world functional tools, replacement parts, brackets, and customized objects using 3D modeling software (CAD) and additive manufacturing printers.

Why It Interests Me: Since I was a kid I was always intrested in the abilities of 3D printing and its potential to create tangible objects from digital designs. As such I want to be able to create my own fuinctional items that can be used in everyday life.

Average Cost: $200 – $600 for an entry-level to mid-range FDM 3D printer, plus $20 – $30 per spool of filament.

Archery

Description: Learning the precision sport of target archery using recurve or compound bows, focusing on form, consistent shooting technique, focus, and target accuracy.

Why It Interests Me: Archery is one of those things you see in sci-fi or fantasy movies, and it always fascinated with how simple it looks but how much skill is required to master it.

Average Cost: $150 – $400 for beginner bow starter kits; $15 – $30 per hour for local range time and rentals.

Boxing

Description: Training in classic boxing fundamentals, including footwork, defensive movement, heavy bag work, pad work, conditioning, and sparring inside a traditional boxing gym.

Why It Interests Me: fighting movies and video games have always intrigued me, and I'm curious to see if I can translate that interest into a real-world skill.

Average Cost: $80 – $180 per month for gym membership; $50 – $120 for essential gear (gloves, hand wraps, mouthguard).

Welding

Description: Mastering the skill of joining metals together using heat and pressure (such as MIG or TIG welding) to fabricate custom metal structures, furniture, and practical repairs.

Why It Interests Me: I want to be able to expand my creativity inusefulitems and welding feels like a cornerstone for achieving that.

Average Cost: $200 – $600 for basic vocational workshop classes; $200 – $500 for entry-level MIG welders and safety gear (helmet, gloves, jacket).
